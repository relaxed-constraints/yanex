# User guide

Follow the workflow that matches your task:

- **Run experiments:** [configuration](running/configuration.md),
  [metrics and artifacts](running/metrics-artifacts.md), [metadata](running/metadata.md),
  [sweeps and staging](running/sweeps.md), [Bash scripts](running/bash-scripts.md),
  or [Python execution](running/programmatic.md).
- **Build pipelines:** [dependencies](pipelines/dependencies.md),
  [dependency sweeps and fan-in experiments](pipelines/sweeps.md), and [lineage](pipelines/graphs.md).
- **Explore results:** [filtering](results/filtering.md), [key syntax](results/keys.md),
  [comparison](results/comparing.md), [Python analysis](results/python.md), and [web UI](results/web-ui.md).
- **Manage data:** [lifecycle operations](managing/lifecycle.md), [sync](managing/sync.md),
  [reproducibility](managing/reproducibility.md), and [storage](managing/storage.md).
- **Improve your workflow:** [automation](automation.md) and [best practices](best-practices.md).
